# Feature Extraction

## Why is it needed?

Raw audio contains millions of samples.

Feature extraction converts raw audio into a compact representation that highlights useful speech information.

## Spectrogram

A spectrogram shows how the frequency content of an audio signal changes over time.

Unlike waveforms, spectrograms make speech patterns easier for AI models to analyze.

## Why it matters

Most modern speech AI systems use spectrogram-like representations before generating speaker embeddings.
