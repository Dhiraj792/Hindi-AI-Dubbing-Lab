# Speaker Embeddings

## Definition

A speaker embedding is a fixed-length numerical vector that represents the unique vocal characteristics of a speaker.

## Purpose

Instead of comparing raw audio waveforms, AI compares speaker embeddings.

Similar speakers produce similar embeddings.

Different speakers produce embeddings that are farther apart.

## Applications

- Speaker Diarization
- Voice Cloning
- Speaker Verification
- Speaker Identification

## Importance for Our Project

Speaker embeddings allow us to consistently assign subtitle lines to the correct speaker and later clone each speaker's voice for Hindi dubbing.
